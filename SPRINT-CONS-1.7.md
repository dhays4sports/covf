# CONS-1.7 — Consultation Outcome and Disposition

## Goal
Add an actionable consultation stage and final outcome to the existing producer workflow.

## Acceptance criteria
- Stage persists for remote and browser-local records.
- Closing requires a supported final outcome.
- Reopening clears the final outcome without deleting history.
- Stage and outcome changes appear in the activity timeline.
- Queue search and filtering include disposition state.
- Existing consultation workflows remain deployable.
