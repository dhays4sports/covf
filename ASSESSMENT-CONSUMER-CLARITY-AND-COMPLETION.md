# ASMT-1.8 — Consumer Clarity and Completion Optimization

CoverageFit now separates consumer-facing presentation from the unchanged assessment and scoring contract. Required property confirmation is shown first, optional home details are collapsed, question and answer copy is simplified at render time, evidence-quality terminology remains in report metadata rather than dominating the homeowner flow, the early insight is an inline non-blocking checkpoint, and the estimated completion time is five minutes.

The presentation layer does not change question keys, weights, answer impacts, finding types, evidence classifications, category scoring, Protection Score calculations, prioritization, report payloads, or consultation records.
