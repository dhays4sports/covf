# CD-1.6 — Decisions and Next Steps

## Objective

Make the existing Consultation Document closeout useful during and after the conversation by carrying forward the structured GC-1.9 Consultation Completion record.

## Implemented

- Reused the existing GC-1.9 completion model and consultation-record persistence.
- Added the saved homeowner decision summary to the canonical Decisions and Next Steps chapter.
- Added explicit unresolved-work status and the producer-recorded detail when items remain open.
- Added formal carrier-quote status and any recorded information, documents, options, or checks still needed.
- Added the existing finding-level Recommendation Builder decisions and verification readiness.
- Added the agreed next action plus the current secure follow-up state, date, and note.
- Added a truthful draft state for unfinished and legacy records, with working context visually separated from completed decisions.

## Guardrails

- The document does not infer homeowner agreement or silently complete a closeout.
- Unresolved work remains open until the producer records otherwise.
- A carrier-quote status is not a proposal, coverage determination, price, eligibility decision, underwriting approval, or issued-policy term.
- No second closeout, scheduling, persistence, recommendation, assessment, or document architecture was created.
